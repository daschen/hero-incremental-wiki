Every system and when it unlocks

On Fire · Unranked

Chain kills without getting hit to build heat. Heat multiplies kill Credits up to 2.5×.

Ultimates · Unranked

Damage and kills charge your hero's ultimate. Press Q when it's ready.

Redeploy · Bronze 5

Cash in Credits for permanent Redeploy points and Rank XP.

Cargo Bay · Bronze 5

Cargo never resets. Spend it (C) on permanent upgrades that boost everything.

Payload · Bronze 4

Escort the payload through the Main Hall. Checkpoints pay Cargo, Credits and Rank XP.

Turrets · Bronze 3

Bots drop Scrap. Build turrets at the glowing sockets to fight alongside you.

Hero Mastery · Bronze 3

Kills level up the hero you play. Pick a minor perk at level 2 and a major perk at level 3.

Bounties · Bronze 3

WANTED bots appear every few minutes. Take them down for Bounty Marks.

Control Point · Bronze 2

Capture the active point for Competitive Points. The point moves after each capture.

Runes · Bronze 2

Stand on an altar in the Armory to roll. Every tier you collect boosts you forever.

Tier Promotion · Bronze 1

Promote to Silver: reset your Redeploy progress for Skill Rating and a new ladder.

Sturmfeste · Silver 5

A storm-lashed castle town whose Sentinels, Knights and Wardens pay far more.

Siege Push · Silver 5

Escort the siege robot to the throne. Checkpoints pay Crowns; your best distance is a permanent Credits boost.

Mobility · Silver 4

Parkour runs pay Momentum. Spend it on double jump, dash, wall-run and the grapple hook.

Research Lab · Silver 3

Spend Crowns on permanent research. The timers keep running while you're offline.

Trials · Silver 2

Challenge runs at the Tourney Yard. Beat the handicap for permanent rewards and automation.

Progression tips

Redeploy early and often

Redeploy points never reset between runs. The first few Redeploys pay back fastest, so don't sit on a big pile of Credits.

Cargo Bay first

Cargo upgrades are permanent and boost every run. Spend Cargo as soon as you have it.

Stay On Fire

Chaining kills without taking a hit builds heat up to 2.5× Credits. Healers and tanks keep the streak alive longest.

Park on an altar

Runes only roll while you stand on an altar (or anywhere with Auto Rune). Idle on one while you plan upgrades.

Tickets reward playtime

You get 1 Ticket every 5 minutes in game. Spend them on permanent Boosts before potions.

Start research before you log off

Research Lab timers keep running while you're offline, so queue the longest project last.

Bounty Marks are permanent

Marks and Competitive Points, and everything you buy with them, never reset.

Every rank is a new hero

Each rank up gives a Hero Token. Try every role: a tank changes how the Payload and Push play.
