Hero Incremental tier list

Which heroes earn Credits fastest once they're upgraded, and the best order to spend your Hero Tokens.

Updated September 26, 2026

Quick answer

S tier: Broker, Bulwark, Solace. They multiply Credits directly or clear whole packs. Unlock Ember first, then Bulwark, Broker, Solace and Rivet as their systems open.

How this tier list works

Heroes are ranked by how quickly they turn bots into Credits at the same upgrade level. Heroes that multiply Credits directly (Broker's Commission and Jackpot, Solace's Sanctuary) or clear whole packs (Bulwark's cleave) rank highest. Splash and bounce damage (Kestrel, Rivet) and grouping tools (Anchor) come next.

Every hero can clear every zone, and tanks and supports make objectives easier. You'll own most of the roster by Silver anyway, since every rank up pays a token.

Tier list FAQ

Who is the best hero in Hero Incremental?

For farming Credits the S tier is Broker, Bulwark, Solace. Broker takes +20% on every kill and his Jackpot makes kills pay 3×; Solace's Sanctuary doubles damage and Credits; Bulwark cleaves whole packs and ignores armor.

What hero should I buy first?

Ember: her 4× fire rate, 120-round tank and burn damage suit the crowded Main Hall at Bronze 5. Then Bulwark at Bronze 4 for the payload.

Is Longshot bad?

He's C tier for farming speed. The 6-round magazine and slow reload lose time between kills. Take the Bandolier perk (+3 magazine) at Mastery 2 if you play him.

Is Vesper good?

Solo she's B tier. Her passive doubles the Squad Bonus to +20% Credits per friend in your server, which makes her S tier when you play with friends.
