Hero Incremental tier list

Which heroes earn Credits fastest once they're upgraded. Every hero can clear every zone; this ranks raw farming speed, not fun.

Updated September 24, 2026

Broker

Jackpot triples kill Credits for 8s, and Mark Asset turns any tough bot into a 3× payout.

Solace

Sanctuary doubles damage and Credits for the whole squad, and lifesteal keeps her On Fire.

Rivet

Ricocheting nails hit two bots at once, and Overclock Field triples your turrets' fire rate.

C

How this tier list works

Heroes are ranked by how quickly they turn bots into Credits at the same upgrade level. Heroes that multiply Credits directly (like Broker's Jackpot or Solace's Sanctuary) or hit several bots at once rank highest. The best farmers right now are Broker, Solace, Rivet.

Tanks and supports also make objectives easier: a tank holds the Payload and Control Point, and healers keep your On Fire streak going. Every rank up gives you a Hero Token, so you'll own most of the roster by Silver anyway. See every hero's kit on the heroes page.
