Hero Incremental currencies

What every currency is for, how to earn it and whether it resets.

Updated September 26, 2026

Quick answer

Credits buy Credit upgrades and rank ups. Redeploy points buy the Redeploy tree. Cargo, Bounty Marks, CP and SR buy permanent trees that never reset, and Silver adds Crowns, Momentum, Honor and Storm Essence.

The layer chain

Each new currency makes the one before it faster. The newest layer is usually the best place to spend time.

Layer milestones

Free nodes for new currencies

The first time you earn 10, 100 and 1,000 of a new currency (Redeploy, Cargo, CP, SR, Crowns, Momentum, Honor, Storm Essence) you get a free upgrade node for it and ×3 of that currency for 10 minutes.

Currency FAQ

What are Bounty Marks used for?

The Bounty shop (F, BOUNTY tab): Skin Vouchers, Ticket Bundles, Mastery Tomes, Bounty Scope (more Marks) and Trophy Hunter (more Credits). Marks never reset.

What are Competitive Points (CP) for?

The Command tree (N): Field Command boosts all rune stats, plus capture speed, cheaper rune rolls and Scrap Magnet. CP never resets.

Does Cargo reset?

No. Cargo and the Cargo Bay are permanent.

How do I get Crowns?

Escort the Siege Push robot in Sturmfeste (Silver 5+): checkpoints pay 20, 45, 80 and 150 Crowns. Crowns also come from day 5 of the daily streak in Silver.

What are layer milestones?

The first time you earn 10, 100 and 1,000 of a new currency (Redeploy, Cargo, CP, SR, Crowns, Momentum, Honor, Storm Essence) you get a free upgrade node for it and ×3 of that currency for 10 minutes.
