Hero Incremental updates

What changed in each build of Hero Incremental, newest first.

Updated September 24, 2026

RunesLatest

Runes, Armory & Shop

  • New Armory room behind the Training Hub with the Rune of Valor and Rune of Fortune altars.
  • Runes rebuilt: stand on an altar to roll. Every tier grows a permanent bonus up to [MAX].
  • Rune of the Tempest altar in the Sturmhorn Tavern. Global Runes give every player in the server free rolls.
  • New Shop with Tickets (1 every 5 minutes you play), permanent Boosts, potions, bundles and codes.
  • Bounty Marks, Competitive Points and their upgrades are now permanent.
  • Roblox Premium members get +10% on every currency.
  • The Control Point is now a ring on the ground that fills as you capture.
Polish

Grapple & feel pass

  • Grapple now lands you on top of perches instead of flinging you off them.
  • Bot shots now come out of their weapons and hands.
  • Capture ring reads upright from any angle.
Roster

Twelve heroes

  • Six new heroes: Anchor, Kestrel, Ember, Rivet, Broker and Vesper.
  • Free choice: every rank up grants a Hero Token for any hero.
  • First-person arms now match your hero's model.
Silver

Sturmfeste

  • Silver ranks unlock Sturmfeste, a storm-lashed castle town with Sentinels, Knights and Wardens.
  • Siege Push, the Mobility tree with parkour courses, the Research Lab and Trials.
  • Fast travel (T) and in-game feedback.
