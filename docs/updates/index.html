Hero Incremental updates

What changed in each update of Hero Incremental, newest first.

Updated September 26, 2026

Overhaul2026-09-26Latest

The big overhaul

  • The Facility is now three sectors, each with its own payload (×1 / ×4 / ×16 rewards) and capture point (×1 / ×5 / ×25 CP).
  • Capture points are long holds against defender waves and pay only when they complete, then relock for 2.5 minutes.
  • New unlock ladder: Turrets at Bronze 3; Runes, capture points and the new Command tree at Bronze 2; Rune of Valor at Bronze 1.
  • Rune altars are free and open by rank. Rolls cost a few Credits each, and Rune Speed, Luck and Bulk are now upgrades in the Credit and Redeploy tabs.
  • Command tree (N): rune stats, capture speed and cheaper rolls with CP; a Scrap branch with Rune Engine and Engineers once you build a turret.
  • Honor Hall (Silver 2) and Tempest Forge (Silver 1) add two more permanent layers.
  • Layer milestones: your first 10, 100 and 1,000 of a new currency give a free node and ×3 of it for 10 minutes.
  • Every hero has a passive, and right-click is now alt-fire: aim, scope, or guard for tanks.
  • Automation is earned in the trees (Quartermaster, Auto-Redeploy, Auto-Capture). New 2× Redeploy, Cargo and Crowns passes, and VIP I–III.
Social2026-09-26

Groups, Hall of Fame & personal objectives

  • Groups of up to 4 (MENU > GROUP): +10% per member, up to +30%.
  • Payloads, capture points and the siege robot are now personal (or shared by your group), so nobody steals your progress.
  • Kill Credits are split by damage dealt, and assists get paid.
  • Hall of Fame wing east of the Hub: 8 all-time boards with statues for #1 and titles for the top 10.
Season 12026-09-24

Stormbreak

  • Season Pass with 30 tiers: free and premium tracks, 3 exclusive skins (Storm Baron, Stormward Seraph, Stormbreaker).
  • 27 hero skins, bought with 600 Tickets or a Skin Token.
  • The Storm Colossus World Boss rises on the Stormfield at the top of every hour.
  • Weekly boards for runes rolled, bots defeated and boss damage, with Ticket rewards.
  • Profile (J) with lifetime stats and titles. The AFK guard rejoins you before the idle kick.
Feel2026-09-26

Effects & polish

  • New effects for every ability and ultimate, bot deaths, rune pulls and rank ups.
  • Damage falls off with range for shotguns and SMGs; out-of-combat health regeneration.
  • Unlock cinematics show off each new system; the dropship flies you to Sturmfeste.
Silver2026-09-24

Sturmfeste

  • Silver ranks open Sturmfeste, a storm-lashed castle town with Sentinels, Knights and Wardens.
  • Siege Push, the Mobility tree and parkour courses, the Research Lab and Trials.
  • Fast travel (T) and in-game feedback.
