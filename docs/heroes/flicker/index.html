How to play Flicker

  • Quantum Reload (Mastery 3) turns every Blink into a free reload.
  • Great for running between sectors and parkour routes, average for raw Credits.
  • Stay inside 18 studs: damage falls off to 40% at range.

Recommended token: Next (#9 in the unlock order). Feels great (1.9× fire rate, +25% speed), but is only average for speed.

Flicker FAQ

Is Flicker good in Hero Incremental?

Flicker is B tier for farming Credits. Solid but nothing extra. Fast and fun to play.

How do I unlock Flicker?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Flicker is Next.

What is Flicker's passive?

Light Feet: +25% movement speed.

What are Flicker's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Fourth Blink or Hollow Points; at Mastery 3 pick Quantum Reload or Afterimage.
