How to play Ember

  • Sweep the stream across a group: burn keeps ticking while you move to the next bot.
  • Kindling's slower heat drain makes staying On Fire easy.
  • Wildfire (Mastery 3) chains through a packed Main Hall.

Recommended token: Bronze 5 (#1 in the unlock order). Best-feeling weapon early: 4× fire rate, a 120-round magazine, and burn keeps killing while you swap targets. The crowded Main Hall suits her short range.

Ember FAQ

Is Ember good in Hero Incremental?

Ember is B tier for farming Credits. Solid but nothing extra. Her burn shines early in the crowded Main Hall.

How do I unlock Ember?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Ember is Bronze 5.

What is Ember's passive?

Kindling: Burning bots take +15% damage from you. On Fire heat drains 50% slower.

What are Ember's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Accelerant or Big Tank; at Mastery 3 pick Wildfire or Scorched Earth.
