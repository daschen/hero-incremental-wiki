How to play Anchor

  • Undertow piles a whole wave into one spot: pair it with any splash hero in a group.
  • Heavy Hauler is the fastest way to push the Silver siege robot.
  • The 26-stud lash hits bots other melee heroes can't reach.

Recommended token: Promotion (#6 in the unlock order). Heavy Hauler makes the Silver siege robot roll 50% faster, and Silver 5 is when the Push opens.

Anchor FAQ

Is Anchor good in Hero Incremental?

Anchor is A tier for farming Credits. Tether and Undertow pull bots into tight groups.

How do I unlock Anchor?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Anchor is Promotion.

What is Anchor's passive?

Heavy Hauler: The Siege Push robot rolls 50% faster while you escort it.

What are Anchor's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Long Chain or Ballast; at Mastery 3 pick Riptide or Maelstrom.
