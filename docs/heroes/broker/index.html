How to play Broker

  • Mark the WANTED bounty bot: it already pays 10× Credits, and the mark triples that.
  • Save Jackpot for a fresh wave so every kill in the 8s window counts.
  • Commission applies to every kill, so he's the best pick on long ranks.

Recommended token: Bronze 3 (#3 in the unlock order). Bounties open now (+25% Marks). Just before the long ranks, +20% on every kill, 3× Mark Asset and the Jackpot ult start to matter. His slow revolver is the trade-off.

Broker FAQ

Is Broker good in Hero Incremental?

Broker is S tier for farming Credits. +20% Credits on every kill, Jackpot makes kills pay 3×, and marked bots pay 3×.

How do I unlock Broker?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Broker is Bronze 3.

What is Broker's passive?

Commission: +20% Credits from kills and +25% Bounty Marks.

What are Broker's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Retainer or Insider Tip; at Mastery 3 pick Hostile Takeover or Windfall.
