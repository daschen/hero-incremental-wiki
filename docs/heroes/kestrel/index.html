How to play Kestrel

Kestrel is a Damage hero in Hero Incremental. Weapon: the Talon Crossbows. Heavy bolts that stick in and burst after a moment, hitting everything nearby. Press E for Grapple Line (7s cooldown) and Q for Talon Dive once your ultimate is charged.

Unlock Kestrel with a Hero Token: you get one every time you rank up, and can spend it on any hero. Other Damage heroes: Vanguard, Flicker, Havoc, Longshot, Ember, Rivet, Broker.
