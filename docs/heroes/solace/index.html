How to play Solace

  • Eternal Sanctuary (Mastery 3) stretches the best burst in the game to 15s.
  • Regeneration lets her hold capture points solo through defender waves.
  • In a group, stand in the middle when you cast: everyone within 30 studs gets 2× Credits.

Recommended token: Bronze 2 (#4 in the unlock order). Capture points open, and she can hold them because she regenerates. Sanctuary gives 2× damage and 2× Credits for 10 seconds, the best burst of income in a 30–70 minute rank.

Solace FAQ

Is Solace good in Hero Incremental?

Solace is S tier for farming Credits. Sanctuary gives 2× damage and 2× Credits for 10 seconds.

How do I unlock Solace?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Solace is Bronze 2.

What is Solace's passive?

Grace: Regenerate 6 HP per second.

What are Solace's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Abundant Grace or Swift Renew; at Mastery 3 pick Vampiric Bolts or Eternal Sanctuary.
