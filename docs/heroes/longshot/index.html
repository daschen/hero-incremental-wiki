How to play Longshot

  • Take Bandolier at Mastery 2: the 6-round magazine is his biggest weakness.
  • Shoot from 40+ studs for the full long-range bonus.
  • Lockdown is great against a full room of tanky Silver bots.

Recommended token: Last (#11 in the unlock order). A 6-round magazine and a slow reload lose too much time.

Longshot FAQ

Is Longshot good in Hero Incremental?

Longshot is C tier for farming Credits. A 6-round magazine and a slow reload cost too much time between kills.

How do I unlock Longshot?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Longshot is Last.

What is Longshot's passive?

Steady Aim: Crits deal 3× instead of 2×.

What are Longshot's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Steady Hands or Bandolier; at Mastery 3 pick Marksman or Headhunter.
