How to play Bulwark

  • Stand on the payload cart: nothing in the Main Hall can out-damage your 250 HP and 20% reduction.
  • Armor pierce makes him the best Armored-bot farmer at Bronze 3.
  • Hold right-click to Guard (−60% damage taken) while you walk a capture point.

Recommended token: Bronze 4 (#2 in the unlock order). The payload unlocks now. He cleaves whole squads, has 250 HP and can stand on the cart. Armor pierce pays off again at Bronze 3 against Armored bots.

Bulwark FAQ

Is Bulwark good in Hero Incremental?

Bulwark is S tier for farming Credits. Cleaves whole packs, ignores armor and can sit on objectives.

How do I unlock Bulwark?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Bulwark is Bronze 4.

What is Bulwark's passive?

Plated: 250 health and 20% damage reduction.

What are Bulwark's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Fortified or Long Barrier; at Mastery 3 pick Long Haft or Aftershock.
