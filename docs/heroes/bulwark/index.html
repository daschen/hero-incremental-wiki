How to play Bulwark

Bulwark is a Tank hero in Hero Incremental. Weapon: the Breaker Hammer. Slow, crushing melee swings that cleave every bot in front of you. Press E for Barrier (14s cooldown) and Q for Seismic Slam once your ultimate is charged.

Unlock Bulwark with a Hero Token: you get one every time you rank up, and can spend it on any hero. Other Tank heroes: Anchor.
