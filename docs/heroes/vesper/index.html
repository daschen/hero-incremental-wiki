How to play Vesper

  • Symbiosis only counts friends in your server: with 3 friends it's +60% Credits.
  • Confluence pierces, so line it up along a payload track.
  • Solo, she's a steady B-tier farmer that never stops to reload.

Recommended token: Next (#10 in the unlock order). 5× fire rate and never reloads. Her Squad Bonus doubles only with friends, so buy her earlier if you play in a group.

Vesper FAQ

Is Vesper good in Hero Incremental?

Vesper is B tier for farming Credits. Her passive only helps with friends in the server. In a group she's S tier.

How do I unlock Vesper?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Vesper is Next.

What is Vesper's passive?

Symbiosis: Your Squad Bonus is doubled: +20% Credits per friend in your server.

What are Vesper's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Potency or Swift Bloom; at Mastery 3 pick Overgrowth or Deep Confluence.
