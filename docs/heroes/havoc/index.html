How to play Havoc

  • Walk into the pack: at 22 studs the scattergun does only 10% damage.
  • Soul Siphon keeps you alive through defender waves on capture points.
  • Executioner (Mastery 3) is his best perk for farming.

Recommended token: Next (#8 in the unlock order). A strong brawler, but you need to be in close.

Havoc FAQ

Is Havoc good in Hero Incremental?

Havoc is A tier for farming Credits. Hits very hard up close and heals 25 HP on every kill.

How do I unlock Havoc?

Spend a Hero Token at the Hero terminal (press H). You get one every rank up; the recommended moment for Havoc is Next.

What is Havoc's passive?

Soul Siphon: Heal 25 HP on every kill.

What are Havoc's best perks?

At Mastery 2 pick Tight Choke or Deep Siphon; at Mastery 3 pick Lingering Wraith or Executioner.
