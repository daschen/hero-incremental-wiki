Hero Incremental FAQ

Short answers to the questions players ask most.

Updated September 26, 2026

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more heroes in Hero Incremental?

Every rank up pays one Hero Token, and the promotion to Silver pays another. Spend tokens at the Hero terminal in the Hub (or press H). You start with Vanguard, so the 11 tokens from Bronze 5 to Silver 5 unlock the rest of the roster. Hero Tokens can also be bought in the Store, and the code THANKYOU gives one.

What should I spend my first Hero Token on?

Ember is the best first pick: 4× fire rate, a 120-round tank and burn damage suit the crowded Main Hall. Then Bulwark at Bronze 4 for the payload and Broker at Bronze 3 for Credits. See the recommended unlock order on the tier list page.

What resets when I rank up?

Credits, Credit upgrades, Redeploy points, the Redeploy tree (except nodes kept by Standing Orders), Scrap and turrets (unless you have Engineers level 2). Cargo, Bounty Marks, CP, SR, runes, heroes, skins and every permanent tree stay.

What does Redeploy do?

Redeploy cashes in your Credits for Redeploy points and Rank XP, then resets your Credits and Credit upgrades. RP buy the Redeploy tree, whose Redeploy Multiplier and Credit Surge double your gains per level.

Do I earn anything while offline?

No. Credits, Tickets and runes only come in while you're in the game. Research Lab timers are the one exception: they keep counting down while you're away.

Will I get kicked for being AFK?

No. The game quietly rejoins you before Roblox's 20-minute idle kick, so you can keep rolling runes or letting payloads creep.

How do rune altars work?

Stand on an open altar and it rolls every second. Each rune costs a few Credits (half a Training kill's worth, less with Rune Rations). Every tier you own gives a permanent bonus that grows with your count up to [MAX]. Altars are free and open by rank.

How do I get to Sturmfeste?

Reach Silver 5 by promoting from Bronze 1. The back door of the Armory opens onto the dropship pad; board it, or use fast travel (T), to reach the Sturmhorn Tavern.

When does the World Boss spawn?

The Storm Colossus rises on the Stormfield at the top of every hour (from Silver 5). You have 5 minutes to bring it down. Everyone who deals at least 1% of its health shares the loot.

How do I play with friends?

Open the Menu (V) and choose GROUP to invite up to 3 friends. A group gets +10% per member (up to +30%), shares its own payloads, capture points and siege robot, and splits kill Credits by damage dealt.

How do I get skins?

Each skin costs 600 Tickets or one Skin Token. Skin Tokens come from day 7 of the daily streak, Skin Vouchers in the Bounty tab, Season Pass tiers and the Store. Skins are cosmetic only.

What are codes and where do I redeem them?

Codes give free rewards. Press X to open the Shop and use the Codes section, or open Settings (gear icon). See the codes page for every working code.

Is VIP worth it?

VIP I (249 Robux) gives ×1.5 damage, fire rate and every currency plus double daily rewards. VIP II and III replace it with ×2 and ×4. Only the highest VIP tier applies. Everything else in the game can be earned by playing.

What is the fastest way to rank up?

Buy Redeploy Multiplier and Credit Surge every rank, Redeploy often, keep the Rune of Fortune rolling, spend Cargo immediately, and play an S-tier farmer (Broker, Bulwark or Solace). Use the rank-up calculator to plan.

Still stuck?

Every system has its own page: currencies, upgrades, objectives, Sturmfeste and events. You can also send feedback in game from the terminal on the Hub wall.
